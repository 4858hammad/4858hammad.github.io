IT Helpdesk Portal

TTI Testing Laboratories · 2026 · Odoo ERP

Odoo 18PortalHelpdesk

About this project

Built an employee helpdesk portal where any portal user can raise an IT ticket pinned to one company. Tickets carry a category, attachments and an expected resolution time, notify the support team by email and can be closed and reopened.

Key features / deliverables

  • Ticket creation for any portal user, pinned to one company, with a team email notification
  • Category, team picker, attachments and expected resolution time on the new-ticket form
  • Submitter and CC list always copied on notifications
  • Close and reopen flow with a redesigned portal view
