About this project

Designed and built the backend and portal frontend for TTI's Odoo 18 employee self-service experience. More than 250 employees across Lahore, Karachi, Sialkot and Faisalabad use the same Odoo environment to access attendance, leave requests, timesheets, tasks, payslips, loans, documents and profile information with role-aware security.

Key features / deliverables

  • Serves 250+ employees across 4 branches
  • Self-service attendance, leave, timesheets, tasks, payslips, loans, documents and profiles
  • Built backend business logic and portal frontend
  • Implemented ACLs, record rules and user-specific visibility

Also available as a NeuralBytea app

A marketplace edition is published as Employee Self Service.
