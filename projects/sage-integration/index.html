Sage–Odoo Accounting Integration

Two-way sync of companies, customers, invoices and payments between Sage and Odoo

About this project

Led a Python/Flask integration that synchronizes companies, customers, invoices and payments between Sage and Odoo Accounting. Field mapping, partial-payment handling, reconciliation, logging and scheduled jobs keep both ledgers consistent.

Key deliverables

  • Synchronized companies, customers, invoices and payments between Sage and Odoo
  • Field mapping and partial-payment handling with reconciliation
  • Logging and scheduled jobs for unattended, auditable sync runs
