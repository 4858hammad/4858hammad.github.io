Field Salesperson Portal

TTI Testing Laboratories · 2026 · Odoo ERP

Odoo 18PortalCRMSalesAccountingSecurityMigration

About this project

Built a self-service field sales portal on Odoo 18 so field reps can work from standard portal accounts. The solution connects visits, meetings, leads, orders, invoices, payments, collections and targets while enforcing territory-aware visibility for salespeople, managers and overseers.

Key features / deliverables

  • Supports ~15–20 field salespeople and 5–7 managers
  • Visit → meeting → CRM lead → order → invoice → payment workflow
  • Territory hierarchy with salesperson, manager and overseer visibility
  • Targets, recovery rate, top customers/products and live sales KPIs
  • Wrote batch migration/backfill logic for 54,000+ historical records and territory mappings

Also available as a NeuralBytea app

A marketplace edition is published as Field Sales Booking.
