Employee Loan Management & Payroll Recovery

TTI Testing Laboratories · 2026 · Odoo ERP

Odoo 18PayrollAccountingOWLPortalLoans

About this project

Built an Odoo 18 Enterprise loan and salary-advance solution supporting TTI's 250+ employee workforce. Configurable loan types provision accounting and payroll records; approval generates disbursement entries and installment schedules; payroll automatically deducts installments; employees track repayment from the portal.

Key features / deliverables

  • Automatic disbursement journal entries and payroll-linked installment recovery
  • Configurable loan types with salary rules, payslip inputs, sequences and account mapping
  • OWL dashboard for disbursed, recovered, outstanding, overdue and recovery analytics
  • Employee portal for repayment progress and paid/overdue/upcoming installments
  • Flexible rescheduling of unpaid installments

Also available as a NeuralBytea app

A marketplace edition is published as Employee Loan Pro.
